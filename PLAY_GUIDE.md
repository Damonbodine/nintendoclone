# Super Mario Bros. Clone - Play Guide

## Controls

| Action       | Primary Key  | Alternate       |
|--------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Move Left    | Arrow Left  | A               |
| Move Right   | Arrow Right | D               |
| Jump         | Z / Space   |                 |
| Run / Fire   | X / L-Shift |                 |
| Crouch       | Arrow Down  | S (Big Mario)   |
| Pause        | Enter       |                 |

**Tips:** Hold the jump button longer for higher jumps. Hold Run while moving to build speed — running jumps cover more distance and height.

---

## Mario's Power States

### Small Mario
- One hit kills you.
- Cannot break bricks or crouch.
- Bouncing off ? blocks awards coins and items.

### Big Mario
- Takes a hit and shrinks back to Small (with brief invincibility).
- Breaks brick blocks from below.
- Can crouch by pressing Down.

### Fire Mario
- All Big Mario abilities plus fireballs.
- Press Run (X / Shift) to throw a fireball (max 2 on screen).
- Fireballs bounce along the ground and destroy enemies on contact.
- Taking a hit downgrades to Big Mario.

---

## Enemies

### Goomba
Brown mushroom-shaped enemies that walk back and forth. Jump on them to squish them (100 pts). Fireballs and shells also work (200 pts).

### Koopa Troopa
Green-shelled turtles. Stomp once to send them into their shell. Touch the idle shell to kick it — a sliding shell plows through other enemies (500 pts each). Be careful: a moving shell can hurt you too.

### Piranha Plant
Red plants that pop in and out of pipes. They won't emerge if you're standing right next to the pipe. Can only be killed with fireballs or star power (200 pts).

## Blocks

- **? Block** — Hit from below to release coins or power-ups. Shimmers until used.
- **Brick** — Small Mario bumps them; Big Mario breaks them. Some hide coins or stars.
- **Multi-Coin Brick** — Looks like a normal brick but dispenses up to 10 coins. Hit it fast before the timer runs out.
- **Hidden Block** — Completely invisible until you jump into them from below. Contains coins or 1-UPs.
- **Used Block** — Already emptied. Solid but boring.

## World 1-1 Layout

The level scrolls left-to-right across 224 tiles (~3584 pixels).

**What to expect:**
1. Flat ground with early ? blocks — learn the controls here.
2. Pipes of varying heights — jump over or onto them.
3. Brick platforms with goombas patrolling on top.
4. Gaps in the ground — fall in and you lose a life, no matter your power state.
5. Staircase sections before and after each pit.
6. A final tall staircase leading to the flagpole — touch it to complete the level.

**Secrets:**
- There's a hidden 1-UP block above the first pit area.
- One brick contains a Starman — grab it for invincibility to plow through the enemies ahead.
- A multi-coin brick near the middle of the level gives up to 10 coins if you keep hitting it.

## Advanced Mechanics

**Jump Buffering** — You can press Jump up to 6 frames before landing and Mario will jump immediately on touchdown. Useful for tight platforming.

**Variable Jump Height** — Tap Jump for a short hop; hold it for full height. Gravity is lower while you hold the button, giving you more air time.

**Speed-Dependent Jumps** — Running jumps use a different gravity curve. Sprint before jumping to reach higher platforms and clear wider gaps.

**Stomp Bounce** — After stomping an enemy, Mario bounces upward. You can chain stomps on consecutive enemies without touching the ground.

**Shell Kicking** — Walk into an idle Koopa shell to launch it. The shell slides across the ground, destroying everything in its path. Stand on the correct side so it launches away from you.

**Damage Invincibility** — After getting hit, you flash for about 2 seconds and can't take further damage. Use this window to get past dangerous spots.

**Star Power** — Color-cycling invincibility. Run through enemies to destroy them all. Lasts about 8 seconds — don't waste it standing still.

## HUD

The top of the screen displays:
- **MARIO** — Your current score (6 digits)
- **COINS** — Coin counter (collect 100 for a 1-UP)
- **WORLD** — Current world (1-1)
- **TIME** — Countdown timer (starts at 400)
- **LIVES** — Remaining lives (start with 3)

---

## Quick Start

1. Launch the game — you'll see the title screen.
2. Press **Enter** or **Z** to start.
3. Run right, jump on enemies, hit ? blocks, avoid pits.
4. Reach the flagpole at the end to clear the level.
5. Have fun.
